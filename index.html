Objectives

  • Visualise interference of coherent light
  • Study how λ, d and D control fringe width
  • Verify β = λD/d numerically
  • Record and compare readings

Key concepts

  • Superposition & coherence
  • Path difference Δ = d sinθ
  • Bright: Δ = mλ · Dark: Δ = (m+½)λ
  • I = Imax cos²(πdy/λD)
