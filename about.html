Introduction & Aim

An interactive web-based simulation of Young's double-slit experiment. Aim: study interference and verify fringe-width relations.

Objectives

  • Simulate interference in real time
  • Apply β = λD/d
  • Record and compare readings

Methodology

Physics in physics.js (SI units) → Canvas renders screen and graph → sliders update all outputs → worksheet and quiz support learning.

Technology

HTML5, CSS3, vanilla JavaScript, Canvas API.

Applications

Wavelength measurement, interferometry, optical coatings, sensors.

Limitations

Ideal coherent equal-intensity slits; no single-slit diffraction envelope; small-angle approximation; the screen window is fixed at ±10 mm.

Future scope

Add the diffraction envelope, white-light fringes, thin-film interference, data export.

Conclusion

The simulation confirms that fringe width increases with λ and D and decreases with d, matching β = λD/d.
